Questions, answered.

What trucks does the O1 fit?

Any flatbed on a 3/4-ton or 1-ton chassis, 6.5 ft bed or longer. We ship a subframe kit matched to your make and model, and mount it at the shop or send it out with instructions.

How long is the wait?

Roughly 7–9 months from deposit to delivery. Slots are released in quarterly batches; the deposit holds your place in the queue and is refundable up to the build start date.

Can I finance it?

Yes. We work with two RV lenders who treat truck campers as recreational vehicles, with terms up to 15 years. Pre-qualification takes about a day.

Is it really four-season?

The shell has no thermal bridges and every tank sits inside the heated envelope. Owners winter-camp it below 0°F on the Overland and Expedition trims.
